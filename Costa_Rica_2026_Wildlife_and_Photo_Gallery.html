Golden Hour & Landscape Compositions by Stop

Compass-verified sunrise, sunset, waterfall, and volcanic steam locations at each of your 4 stays.

Stop 1 • Potrero & North Coast (Nov 16–18) 24–70mm + CPL + ND Filter

Over-Water Bay Sunrise & Volcanic Headland Sunsets

🌅 Sunrise (5:15–6:15 AM) — East Side of Flamingo Peninsula / Marina Overlook: Because Flamingo Peninsula juts north-northwest, its eastern shoreline looks East-Southeast across calm Potrero Bay toward the inland volcano chain—giving you a rare sunrise over the water on the Pacific coast with moored sailboats in the foreground.

🌇 Sunset (4:45–5:35 PM) — Lux. Romantic Cocoon Deck, Playa Penca & Las Catalinas Ridge: Capture storm-cloud reflections right from your cliffside plunge pool, low-tide mirror sand framed by dark volcanic rocks at Playa Penca (5 mins away), or elevated island views over the Catalina Islands from Playa Danta ridge trail.

Stop 2 • Sámara, Carrillo & Camaronal (Nov 18–20) 70–200mm + 16–35mm

Sea Turtle Tracks at Dawn & Coconut-Palm Bay Compression

🌅 Sunrise (5:20–6:30 AM) — Playa Camaronal Wildlife Refuge & Buena Vista Estuary: Drive 15 mins south of Carrillo to Camaronal for wild volcanic sea stacks, crashing surf, and Olive Ridley sea turtle nesting tracks and hatchlings in warm dawn sidelight—or walk 4 mins from Gecko Verde to wild Playa Buenavista.

🌇 Sunset (4:45–5:35 PM) — Mirador de Playa Carrillo & West End Reef: Stop at the high cliffside overlook on Route 150 between Sámara and Carrillo with a 70–200mm lens to compress hundreds of leaning coconut palms framing the bay, then walk onto the wet sand at Carrillo's west end for mirror sky reflections.

Stop 3 • Bijagua, Bitzu Dome & Río Celeste (Nov 20–22) Tripod + CPL + 100–400mm + Macro

Neon Turquoise Waterfalls, Private River Pools & Volcano Mist

🌅 Sunrise (5:30–6:30 AM) — Bitzu Dome Miravalles Trail & Route 6 Pastures: Walk the private 1 km Miravalles viewpoint trail right next to Bitzu Dome as golden morning light cuts through valley fog below the twin peaks of Tenorio Volcano.

💧 Waterfalls (8:00 AM) — Río Celeste + Private Cataratas Bijagua Trail: Enter Tenorio National Park at 8:00 AM sharp with a circular polarizer (CPL) to cut glare off the neon-blue pool (`1/4s–1s`, ISO 64–100). Back at Bitzu Dome, walk 150m to the private 2 km Cataratas Bijagua waterfall trail for crowd-free long exposures.

Stop 4 • Rincón de la Vieja & Río Negro (Nov 22–23) 24–105mm + Fast Prime

Backlit Volcanic Fumaroles, Canyon Waterfalls & Savanna Sunset

🌋 Morning Steam (10:15 AM) — Las Pailas Volcanic Loop & Oropéndola Waterfall: Shoot into angled morning sunlight to backlight the rising steam plumes, bubbling clay pots (pailas), and twisted strangler figs, then frame the 82-foot Oropéndola Waterfall plunging into its turquoise canyon.

🌇 Sunset (4:50–5:40 PM) — Hacienda Guachipelín Pacific Savanna: The western slopes of Rincón de la Vieja open across golden Guanacaste savannas looking straight out to the Pacific Ocean and Papagayo Peninsula for fiery crimson last-night skies.
