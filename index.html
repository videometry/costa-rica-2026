  • 4:45–5:30 PM: First sunset photography session right from your private plunge pool overlooking Potrero Bay, followed by beachfront dinner at Coco Loco or Hemingway's.
Day 2 • Tue, Nov 17 Local Hops: 5–20 min

Over-Water Bay Sunrise, Playa Penca & Las Catalinas

  • 5:15–6:15 AM (Sunrise): East side of Flamingo Peninsula / Marina Overlook (captures the sun rising over Potrero Bay water toward the inland volcanoes).
  • 8:30 AM – 12:00 PM: Swim and explore pale-sand Playa Penca & Playa Prieta (5 mins) or white-shell Playa Conchal (20 mins).
  • Afternoon: Lunch in car-free Mediterranean village of Las Catalinas + afternoon plunge pool relax.
  • 4:45–5:30 PM (Sunset): Low-tide reflections at Playa Penca or Las Catalinas Ridge Trail overlooking the Catalina Islands.
Day 3 • Wed, Nov 18 Drive: 2 hr 15 min (Paved)

Inland Drive to Sámara, Gecko Verde & Wild Scarlet Macaws

  • 10:00 AM: Check out of Potrero and take paved Route 21 → Santa Cruz → Nicoya → Route 150.
  • 11:30 AM (Lunch): Stop at Coopetortilla in Santa Cruz for handmade wood-fired cheese tortillas & casados.
  • 2:30–3:00 PM: Check into El Pequeño Gecko Verde—pay ~$250 cash for the 8% discount!
  • 3:45–5:30 PM (Macaws & Sunset): Drive 8 mins to Playa Carrillo to photograph wild Scarlet Macaws in the beach almond trees, then capture sunset from Mirador de Playa Carrillo.
Day 4 • Thu, Nov 19 Local Hops: 5–15 min

Camaronal Sea Turtle Sunrise, Isla Chora Kayak & Carrillo Palms

  • 5:20–6:45 AM (Sunrise): Drive 15 mins south to Playa Camaronal Wildlife Refuge for volcanic sea stacks and Olive Ridley sea turtle tracks/hatchlings—or walk 4 mins from Gecko Verde to wild Playa Buenavista for howler monkeys & coatis.
  • 8:30–11:30 AM: Ocean kayak & snorkel to pink-sand Isla Chora OR flatwater Río Ora mangrove wildlife kayak.
  • Afternoon & Sunset: Swim under the coconut palms at Playa Carrillo + sunset drinks at Hula Jungla.
Day 5 • Fri, Nov 20 Drive: 3 hr 00 min

Coast to Rainforest Valley (Sámara → Bitzu Riverside Dome)

  • 9:30 AM: Check out of Sámara and head inland via Nicoya & Route 18 (La Amistad Bridge) to Highway 1 at Cañas.
  • 12:00–1:30 PM (Guarded Lunch Stop): Stop at Café & Macadamia right at the Hwy 1 / Route 6 turnoff in Cañas (watched parking lot right outside the window + optional 45-min walk at Las Pumas Rescue Center next door).
  • 2:30 PM: Check into Bitzu Dome on the Bijagua River; walk the private 1 km Miravalles viewpoint trail or 2 km Cataratas Bijagua waterfall trail 150m away!
Day 6 • Sat, Nov 21 Local Hops: 10–20 min

Río Celeste Waterfall (8 AM) + Guaranteed Sloth & Frog Walks

  • 8:00–11:15 AM (Río Celeste Hike): Enter Tenorio National Park on the first 8:00 AM SINAC slot ahead of tour buses to photograph the neon-turquoise waterfall, Blue Lagoon & El Teñidero.
  • 11:30 AM: Lunch right outside the park at Tapirus Paradise (active hummingbird & tanager feeders on the deck!).
  • 2:00–3:30 PM (100% Guaranteed Sloths): Guided walk at Finca Verde Lodge ($15/pp) for eye-level two-toed and three-toed sloths in low cecropia trees + strawberry poison dart frogs.
  • 5:30–7:30 PM (Twilight Walk): Dusk walk at Tapir Valley Nature Reserve for Red-Eyed Tree Frogs, Glass Frogs & wild Baird's Tapirs.
Day 7 • Sun, Nov 22 Drive: 1 hr 45 min

Las Pailas Volcano Loop, Oropéndola Waterfall & Río Negro Hot Springs

  • 7:45 AM: Depart Bijagua down Route 6 → Hwy 1 → Curubandé to Hacienda Guachipelín.
  • 9:45 AM (Drop Bags First!): Pre-check in at Guachipelín reception (present physical credit card used to pay!), get your hot springs/waterfall wristbands, and lock bags with the bell desk.
  • 10:15 AM – 12:30 PM: Hike the 3 km Las Pailas volcanic mud pots & fumaroles loop (open Sundays; closed Mondays!) + visit 82-ft Oropéndola Waterfall.
  • 2:30–5:35 PM: Soak in Río Negro Hot Springs & volcanic mud baths (included!) + capture sunset over the Guanacaste volcanic savanna.
Day 8 • Mon, Nov 23 Drive: 45–50 min

Ranch Morning Wildlife, Buffet Breakfast & Easy Drive to LIR

  • 6:30–8:30 AM: Morning bird & monkey walk around the ranch cenízaro trees, followed by your included full Costa Rican buffet breakfast.
  • 9:15 AM: Check out and drive 45 minutes on paved roads via Curubandé & Liberia to Enterprise Car Rental.
  • 10:30 AM: Return Suzuki Jimny and hop the 5-minute Enterprise shuttle to LIR Terminal for your ~2:00 PM Delta flight home.
